Joining a shared entrepreneurship network

Who signs: Every entrepreneur, once, when their record first enters the shared network.

The plain-language explanation of what a federated network is and what joining it means. This is the document that has to be understandable, not just correct.

You are joining something shared by design

This system is part of a federated network of independent entrepreneurship-support organizations. Each organization runs its own tools and keeps control of its own data, and organizations that have signed the shared privacy and standards compact can exchange information about the entrepreneurs they support — with your consent.

What becomes visible across organizations

Your core directory information — name, contact, venture name, and a short description — becomes visible to organizations in this ecosystem so they can find and support you. Deeper data (interaction notes, program details, financials) stays with the organization that recorded it unless you explicitly approve cross-organization sharing.

You stay in control of what moves

Data sharing between specific organizations requires your consent each time a new organization asks for access. You can revoke access at any time from your privacy settings. Revoking your participation here does not affect your membership or relationship with any individual organization.

Why this is better than a central database

No single organization holds everyone's data. Each ecosystem participant keeps control of its own records, and we agree on shared standards so your information can move between organizations with your permission rather than being duplicated or locked behind one system.

What this organization will not do

We do not sell your data, we do not share it outside the compact without your consent, and we do not use it for purposes unrelated to entrepreneurship support. Organizations violating the compact can be removed from the network.

Entrepreneur Data & Privacy Notice

Who signs: Every entrepreneur, alongside the compact.

The specifics: what is stored, who can see it, how cross-organization sharing is approved, and what rights the entrepreneur keeps.

What information is stored

When you participate in ecosystem programs, support organizations (ESOs) may record your basic profile (name, contact info, venture name and description), program participation and progress milestones, and interaction notes related to the services they provide to you.

Who can see your data

Each ESO only sees records they have created or that you have explicitly shared with them. Your information is not visible to organizations you have not worked with. Basic directory information (name, venture, contact) is visible to all ESOs in this ecosystem.

Cross-organization sharing

If you work with multiple ESOs and want them to coordinate, you must explicitly approve any cross-organization data sharing. You will always be asked before a second organization can access another organization's notes or records about you.

Your rights

You can view which organizations have access to your data from your Privacy Settings at any time. You can restrict or revoke access to your data, and you can request a copy or deletion of your records by contacting your ecosystem administrator.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data to third parties. We do not share your information outside this ecosystem without your consent. Individual interaction notes are confidential between you and the recording organization.

Data Access & Responsibility Agreement

Who signs: Every member organization, and its staff who access shared data.

The obligations an organization takes on in exchange for access — the counterpart to what entrepreneurs are promised above.

Permitted uses

You may access entrepreneur information solely to provide ecosystem support services: reviewing venture details to offer relevant programs, recording interaction notes and program participation, and coordinating referrals with the entrepreneur's explicit consent.

Confidentiality obligations

You must treat all entrepreneur data as confidential. Do not share venture details, financials, interaction notes, or any personally identifiable information outside this platform or with parties not authorized to access it through this system.

Prohibited activities

You may not use entrepreneur data for purposes unrelated to providing ecosystem support. You may not share data externally, use it for commercial gain, or access records beyond what your role requires. You may not attempt to access data belonging to organizations outside your assigned scope.

Audit and accountability

All data access and modifications are logged and may be reviewed by ecosystem administrators. Access is scoped to your assigned organization and role. Administrators may audit activity at any time.

Reporting obligations

If you become aware of an accidental data disclosure or potential breach, report it immediately to your organization administrator. Any observed misuse of entrepreneur data should be reported through the platform feedback system.

Consequences of violation

Unauthorized use or disclosure of entrepreneur data may result in immediate access revocation, removal from this ecosystem, and potential civil or legal liability. This agreement survives the end of your participation in this ecosystem.
